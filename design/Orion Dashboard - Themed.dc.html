OBSERVED CONVENTIONS

Disciplines & directions

How the work is built and why — read from your instruction & design documents, never authored here. Meant to make the work legible to an outside reader.

Global

across all projects
Local-first by default

Data and tooling run on the developer's own machine; hosting is an outer layer, not the source of truth.

observed · docs/orion-strategy.md
Untrusted text is inert

Commit messages, task names and comments are always rendered as plain text — a hard security rule, not a preference.

observed · docs/security.md

orion

project-specific
Observe & reframe, never originate

Plans, tasks and ideas are written in their own places. Orion reads and reframes them — it is never an authoring tool.

observed · docs/orion-strategy.md
Sectioned & extensible

The home is distinct top-level sections, not one flat list — so new kinds of content slot in without a redesign.

observed · plans/orion-plan.md
State legible without colour alone

Every status pairs a glyph and a label with its colour, so meaning survives for a colour-blind or plain reading.
